# Scaur logo brief (prompt)

Paste everything below the line into the design tool. Ask for SVG output; if the tool only makes
images, use the result as a direction and have the final redrawn as vector.

---

Design a logo system for **Scaur**, a fictional US brand of technical mountain apparel.

**The brand.** Scaur (pronounced "skar", an old northern English and Scots word for a steep,
rocky crag) sells outdoor clothing as a **layering system**: every product is one of four layers,
base, mid, insulation or shell, and each is rated on one shared temperature scale (-10°F to
80°F) so the layers add up to a kit. Its online store is a headless Shopify storefront whose
signature features are a **kit builder** (you pick the activity and weather, it assembles one
piece per layer) and an **illustrative thermal view** of the products. Customers are hikers,
climbers and trail runners who care about how things work, not hype. The tone is precise,
technical and calm: an instrument, not a lifestyle brand.

**The idea to express.** Four layers forming one system, and/or the crag the name comes from.
Strong routes to explore (show at least three, clearly different):
1. Four stacked bands or strata that together form a crag or an abstract "S".
2. A letterform "S" built from four parallel strokes, like layers wrapping a body.
3. A crag profile cut by horizontal layer lines, reduced to the fewest possible shapes.

**The visual system it must sit in.**
- UI colours: ink `#111418`, frost `#eef1f3`, snow `#f8f9fa`, and one accent, ember `#cf3a1c`.
- A thermal colour scale (deep violet to magenta to orange to pale yellow) exists but is
  reserved for data. **Do not use gradients or thermal colours in the logo.**
- Type: the wordmark is **SCAUR** in Archivo, expanded width (112.5%), ExtraBold 800, uppercase,
  letter-spacing +0.04em. Data type is Martian Mono (not in the logo).
- Flat design: no shadows, glows, bevels, 3D, glass or textures. Square, decisive shapes; the
  site uses square photos and pill-shaped buttons.

**Constraints.**
- Geometric and built on a simple grid; as few shapes as possible.
- Works in one colour (ink on light, white on ink) and in ink plus ember.
- The mark alone must stay recognisable at **16px** as a browser favicon.
- No text inside the mark, no mountain-with-sun, compass, snowflake or pine-tree clichés.
- Must not resemble existing outdoor brands: Arc'teryx (fossil bird), Patagonia (mountain
  skyline), The North Face (half dome with lines), Mammut, Black Diamond, Rab, Norrøna, Salomon.
- Timeless rather than trendy; it should look like it belongs on a sewn-in label and a zip pull.

**Deliverables (SVG, clean filled paths, integer viewBox, no strokes, no embedded images).**
1. Mark alone, on a square grid.
2. Horizontal lockup: mark + SCAUR wordmark.
3. Stacked lockup.
4. One-colour versions: ink on frost, white on ink.
5. Favicon versions at 16, 32 and 48px (simplified if needed, on an ink square).
6. App icon at 180 and 512px (mark on ink or ember ground).
7. A usage sheet: clear space (one layer-band height), minimum sizes, do and don't examples,
   and the lockup on the 1200×630 social card (frost ground, ink type).

For each route, explain in one sentence how it expresses "four layers, one system".
