## Direction contract

THESIS: Scaur shows what a layer does, not only what it looks like. Any product or kit flips into a thermal view on one shared temperature axis. It refuses the category default: a full-bleed ridge photo, "Shop now", and a four-up white grid of jackets that all look alike.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light ground (frost #eef1f3, snow #f8f9fa), ink #111418. The ironbow thermal scale (deep violet to magenta to ember to amber to near-white) appears only where it encodes warmth: ratings, the thermal view, the kit builder. One action colour, ember #cf3a1c. Archivo with its width axis (expanded, heavy for display; normal for text) and Martian Mono for ratings, sizes and prices. Buttons and chips are full pills; media and panels are square. Depth comes only from flat overlapping layers: no drop shadows, blur or glass. Photography is cropped hard onto the garment on the body.

STORY: The visitor sees a layer and its thermal view, understands that Scaur sells a system of four layers rated for conditions, picks their conditions, gets a kit that fits together in the right sizes, adds it in one action, and hands off to checkout.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px bar with the logo, Shop, Kit builder and Search, and a cart count. Left five columns: a two-line headline, a subline of 20 words or fewer, "Build your kit" (ember pill) and a secondary "Shop layers". Right seven columns: a hard-cropped photo of a runner in the shell with a Photo / Thermal switch on it; in thermal mode the image gradient-maps to the ironbow scale and a temperature axis runs along its bottom edge. Directly below the fold: the four-layer strip (base, mid, insulation, shell), each showing its warmth on the shared axis.

FORM: Thermal imaging (the ironbow scale as a functional data layer over a light, photo-led store); position 4 of 6 on the ordered list; seed key 5c35ff05, re-roll 1. Raises: depth by overlap only (from Łowicz papercut); one temperature axis shared by every product (from Horizon Grid); four fixed kit slots that never wrap, empty slots inviting a pick (from Step Sequencer); adding a layer visibly pushes warmth outward (from Floated Pigment); choosing a colourway floods the product stage in one hard cut (from Title Card). Signature interaction: the Photo / Thermal switch, and the kit builder's warmth spread. Motion grammar: decisive cuts for state changes; warmth spreads with an exponential ease-out; reduced motion swaps states instantly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
